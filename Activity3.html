Begginer simple GYM plan

Workout Structure

Start your fitness journey with a simple, full-body gym routine three times a week, focusing on foundational strength exercises and progressive overload.

Begin with machines or light free weights to learn proper form, then gradually progress to dumbbells and barbells. Focus on compound movements that target multiple muscle groups

This is the example for weekly plan

Monday:

Full-body strength (squats, bench press, lat pulldown, shoulder press, planks)

Tuesday:

Cardio (20–30 minutes moderate intensity)

Wednesday

Full-body strength (leg press, rows, chest press, dumbbell shoulder press, core work)

Thursday:

Rest or light activity

Friday:

Full-body strength (lunges, deadlifts, pull-downs, push-ups, planks)

Saturday/Sunday:

Rest or optional light cardio
